    How to read this map

    The colours are quantiles. Boroughs are sorted and divided into groups of equal size, so each shade holds roughly the same number of boroughs. Equal steps in colour are not equal steps in value. The strip under the menu shows where each borough sits and where the class boundaries fall.

    Grey means not reported. Six boroughs did not submit at least one quarterly return in the year to March 2026; Ealing submitted one of four.

    Each measure says whether it is official statistics or an estimate. Household counts, length of stay, children and placements outside the borough are MHCLG statutory returns. Rates are estimates derived from each council's published payment data and have not been confirmed against an invoice.

    A blank rate means no usable figure. Where a council's single payments cover several units, no per-night rate can be derived, and none is shown. The reason is given in the tooltip and in the borough's own figures.

    Why this is a shared problem

    Method and confidence

    Household counts, length of stay, children and placements outside the borough are MHCLG statutory returns: official statistics, not modelled. Length of stay is the time already spent by households present at the quarter end, not the length of a completed stay. Rates are estimates derived from councils' published payment files and have not been confirmed against an invoice.

    What this map does not show, and why

    It does not rank boroughs on published spend. Each borough's published file captures a different and unknowable share of its bill. Published spend per household-night ranges from £3 to £107 across London, which measures publication practice rather than procurement. What can be concluded: a borough's own total over its own period. What cannot: which borough spends more.

    It does not compare an operator's price between boroughs unless both payments sit under the same homelessness label. Without that, one firm's payments mix homelessness lets with children's semi-independent placements, which are different products at different prices.

    It does not show a rate where single payments cover several units. Such a payment cannot be divided into a per-night rate.

      Corrections and clarifications

      Version 1.2, 3 September 2026. The annualised spend figure in each borough's panel has been withdrawn: it understated seven boroughs, Redbridge by 44%, because stray invoice dates inflated the month count. It is replaced by the sum of published payments over the period the files cover, which differs by borough and is shown beside the figure. Ealing's change in median stay has been withdrawn: one quarter reported. "Placed outside the borough" now reads "outside the borough", since the MHCLG total includes households owed a duty with no placement yet made.

      Version 1.1, 3 September 2026. Havering's leased rate withdrawn from the leasing comparison: it is recorded under the leasing cost code but priced per night. The borough panel keeps the figure with that explanation. Two caveat sentences reworded for a public reader.
